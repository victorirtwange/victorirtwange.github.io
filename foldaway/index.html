Hoard tabs, guilt-free.

A calm new tab page for Chrome. One click puts your open tabs away into tidy folders; another brings any of them back.

Add to Chrome Free, with no account

One click

Put your tabs away

Forty tabs you'll read later? A hundred? Put them away: each one is saved into a folder, named after its tab group or sorted by site, and closed, so your tab strip only holds what you're using now.

Or put away just the tab you're on (right-click the page), the ones you tick, or drag a single tab into a folder.

Folders

Bring any back in a click

Folders show their tabs' icons, like app folders on a phone. Open one tab or the whole folder, or search: Foldaway finds any tab you've saved and tells you which folder it's in.

Put something away by mistake? Undo puts tabs back exactly where they were, in the same window, place and tab group.

Import

Bring your tabs along

Coming from OneTab, Toby or Session Buddy? Paste OneTab's list, or import their export files or any browser's bookmarks, and your tabs arrive as folders.

Your page

Make it yours

It looks like Chrome's own new tab page, or pick light, grey or dark, a soft gradient, or a picture of your own, whose colours Foldaway can take on.

Add a clock, a second clock for another place, and the weather for your city, if you'd like them.

And the little things

Idle tabs sleep
Tabs you haven't used for a while hand their memory back, and big folders reopen without loading every tab at once.
What matters stays open
Pinned tabs, tabs playing sound, video calls and the sites you choose are never put away.
Keyboard first
Alt+T opens your folders, Alt+Shift+O puts everything away, and every part works with a screen reader.
Backups that keep up
Export to a file, or keep a copy in your Chrome bookmarks that updates itself, and syncs if Chrome sync is on.
Beside your other new tab
Already using another new tab extension? Foldaway's toolbar button still opens your folders.
Quick with a big hoard
Tested with 20,000 saved tabs, and still quick to open.

Your tabs stay on your computer

Foldaway has no server and asks for no account. Your folders live in Chrome's storage on your computer, and in your Chrome bookmarks only if you turn that on.
